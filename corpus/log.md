# Log

## [2026-10-21] decision | The repo starts: the office apps leave imbatranimOS

The owner created the repo after a grilling held in the imbatranimOS corpus (its `corpus/wiki/decisions-office-suite.md`). The ArtCraft crafts were not brought into the OS as sandboxed canvases (OS brief 161, superseded). Instead the OS's office apps move here: Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDF (PDF Viewer and norPDF merged) and a new CAD app, in TypeScript with editing in the browser, installed by one suite descriptor in the OS repo, and rendered with the desktop's own React and kit. The crafts stay as the behaviour reference. The calls, from this side, are in [decisions.md](wiki/decisions.md); the vocabulary in [glossary.md](wiki/glossary.md). The owner picked the name after openBureau turned out to be taken.

## [2026-10-21] todo | Corpus bootstrapped; briefs 01–06 filed

The corpus follows the imbatranimOS one (same lint, same rules) with this repo's invariants in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): AGPL-3.0, TypeScript only, no WebAssembly in the apps, the shared kit, the crafts as reference, no hosted CI. [craft-reference.md](wiki/craft-reference.md) maps each app to its craft and names the parity documents to read. Six briefs: [01](briefs/todo/01-scaffold-and-a-smoke-app.md) the scaffold, the kit snapshot, the externals and a smoke app; [02](briefs/todo/02-docs-moves-in.md) Docs moved with a parity checklist; [03](briefs/todo/03-cad-a-2d-drafting-app.md) CAD, spike-gated, with a library shortlist (DXF read via dxf-parser or dxf-viewer's parser, write via @tarikjabiri/dxf, geometry via @flatten-js/core and clipper2-ts, rbush, our own Canvas 2D renderer) and DWG out of scope because every browser reader needs wasm; [04](briefs/todo/04-slides-that-edit.md) Slides editing, spike-gated on saving by patching the package in place (pptxtojson as a reading aid, PptxGenJS for new decks, PPTist as a UX reference); [05](briefs/todo/05-sheets-moves-in.md) Sheets moved; [06](briefs/todo/06-one-pdf-app.md) one PDF app whose first page must be as fast as PDF Viewer's. The OS side is imbatranimOS briefs 162, 163 and 164.
